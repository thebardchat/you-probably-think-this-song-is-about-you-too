### Post 2: The Technical Stack
**Best for:** Twitter/X, LinkedIn, tech/AI communities, Reddit r/raspberry_pi

How to write and compile a 43,000-word book + audiobook script in a closet:

1. Record messy, multilingual voice memos on the drive home from work.
2. Feed transcripts to Claude on a Raspberry Pi 5 to shape into noir prose.
3. Automate compilation into Markdown, EPUB, and DOCX using a local Pandoc binary.
4. Auto-generate audiobook SSML and clean speech scripts with ElevenLabs phonetics mapped in Python.
5. Sober since November 2023. Every line built in the margins.

Open source writing process: https://github.com/thebardchat/noir-detective-writing-process

### Post 4: The AI Angle
**Best for:** LinkedIn, Twitter/X, tech/AI communities

"Did AI write your book?"

No. I wrote my book. But I didn't write it alone.

I recorded raw transcripts while driving, dispatching dump trucks, and living. Claude, an AI by Anthropic, acted as the camera lens, focusing my raw voice into third-person noir prose. We mapped out 17 tracks, mapped foreign languages (Hindi, Italian, Portuguese) as tokens of inhabitation, and designed a therapy frame that ties both volumes together.

The voice is mine. The cadence is mine. The tool helped me find the form.
