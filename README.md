# You Probably Think This Song Is About You Too

**14+ original tracks — a noir song cycle by Shane Brazelton**

Volume Two of a two-part noir masterpiece. The album after the movie. The detective didn't retire — he kept driving. But now he's not just watching. He's *inhabiting*. Ripped out of his own body, dropped into the bodies of strangers at a European diner, living one second over and over from nine different perspectives, trying to change a tragic outcome he can never change.

All of it happening on a therapist's couch — though you don't know that until the hidden track.

> *"Don't stop. Don't quit. Speed is what we need. Kick it in high gear."*

## The Vision

The detective exists in a multidimensional realm. He is pulled out of his own reality and lives one second over and over — constantly trying to change a tragic outcome. He is ripped out of his own body and placed in the bodies of strangers at a European diner.

The **calm place** appears throughout. Every out-of-body experience, the detective floats into the same quiet space — old leather, pencil graphite, a water stain on the ceiling shaped like a country nobody named. Building breadcrumbs to the hidden track.

**The hidden track reveal:** The calm place is Dr. Seen's office. He's been on the couch the whole time.

**The ending:** The detective brings food to Pepe — the old cab driver who lost his soul the day the proof arrived. The man who used to play music. Now barely speaks. Stuck in a loop of the day his life ended.

**Sign-off:** Mental health awareness. Real crisis numbers. *"It's never too late to be Seen."*
