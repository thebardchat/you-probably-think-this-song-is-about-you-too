The Vision

The detective exists in a multidimensional realm. He is pulled out of his own reality and lives one second over and over — constantly trying to change a tragic outcome. Ripped out of his own body. Dropped into strangers at a European diner. Nine perspectives. One second. One outcome he cannot change.

The calm place appears throughout. Old leather. Pencil graphite. A water stain shaped like a country nobody named. Building breadcrumbs to the hidden track.

Hidden track reveal: The calm place is Dr. Seen's office. He's been on the couch the whole time.

Sign-off: Mental health awareness. Real crisis numbers. "It's never too late to be Seen."
