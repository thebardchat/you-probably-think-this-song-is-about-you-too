The story

He is everyone and no one at the same time. That's not a riddle. That's the whole album.

The detective didn't retire — he kept driving. But now he's not just watching. He's inhabiting. Ripped out of his own body and dropped into the bodies of strangers at a European diner, living one second over and over from nine different perspectives, trying to change a tragic outcome he can never change.

All of it happening on a therapist's couch — though you don't know that until the needle drops on the hidden track.

The calm place

Every time he leaves his body, he floats into the same quiet room. Old leather. Pencil graphite. A water stain on the ceiling shaped like a country nobody named.

It shows up on every track — a breadcrumb you don't notice you're collecting. And then the hidden track drops the needle on the truth of it.
